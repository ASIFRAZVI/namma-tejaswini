FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common questions

Everything you need to know before reaching out.

Are your products available on GeM? +
Yes — all our products across HP, HPE, Dell, Lenovo, Acer, Epson, Kyocera, Canon, Samsung, LG, Panasonic, APC, Brother, Xerox, and more are listed on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) portal. Government departments can directly raise purchase orders through GeM.
Do you provide AMC (Annual Maintenance Contracts)? +
Yes, we offer comprehensive AMC services for all IT equipment — servers, storage, networking gear, UPS, printers, laptops, desktops, and more. Our AMC plans include preventive maintenance, priority support, and on-site response for Bangalore and surrounding Karnataka districts.
What is your delivery timeline for procurement orders? +
We maintain optimized supply chain partnerships with all major OEMs to ensure minimum turn-around time. Standard orders are typically fulfilled within 7–15 business days depending on the product. For urgent government requirements, expedited timelines can be arranged.
Do you handle datacenter setup and IT infrastructure projects? +
Absolutely. We provide end-to-end datacenter solutions including server racks, precision cooling, UPS and battery systems, electrical panels, raised flooring, biometric access, surveillance cameras, and structured cabling. Our team handles complete turnkey datacenter build-outs.
Do you serve clients outside Bangalore? +
Yes. While our office is in Bangalore, we serve clients across Karnataka — including Davangere, Madikeri, Hosapete, Chithradurga, Ramanagara, Chikkamagaluru, and more. Remote infra management and cloud services extend our reach nationwide.
