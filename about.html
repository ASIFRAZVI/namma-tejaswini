OUR STORY

From vision to
trusted partner

NTSL was founded with a clear belief: that technology can simplify the lives of every citizen and empower organizations to achieve more. Our promoters — seasoned IT professionals — identified a gap in the market for a reliable, transparent, and technically superior IT partner in the government procurement space.

Today, we serve 20+ government departments across Karnataka, central agencies, universities, medical institutions, and private enterprises — all through the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) platform and direct procurement channels.
